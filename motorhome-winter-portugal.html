What the app shows a motorhome

Dump stations

Filter for dump stations and you also get the campsites, caravan sites, fuel stations and car parks that have one. Where it is recorded, each says whether it takes grey water and the chemical toilet, whether there is fresh water, and whether it is for customers only.

LPG

Search for LPG, GPL or autogas and the map brings up fuel stations. The ones recorded as selling LPG say so on their page.

Somewhere to stop

Caravan sites and aires have a filter of their own. A campsite's page says whether it takes motorhomes and caravans, where that is recorded.

The weeks in between

Drinking water, showers, launderettes, supermarkets, pharmacies and free Wi-Fi are on the same map, for when you stay in one place for a while.

Dump stations and LPG in Portugal: what to know before the winter

Many motorhomes spend the winter in the Algarve, and more travel on along the Alentejo coast and through the centre. The questions on the road are practical ones: where can I empty the tanks, where is fresh water, and which fuel station has LPG, sold in Portugal as GPL.

Portugal restricts overnight stays in motorhomes outside designated places, above all on the coast and in protected areas, so knowing where the caravan sites and campsites are matters more than in many countries. Check the local signs, and before you drive to a place, make sure it is still there: the map shows what has been recorded, and things change.
