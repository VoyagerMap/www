4.4 Reports and contributing them to OpenStreetMap

VoyagerMaps builds on OpenStreetMap, a public map database maintained by volunteers. When you report that a place has changed — that it closed, that its opening hours are different, that a car park now charges — or when you propose a new place, that report is useful to everyone who uses that map, not only to us.

By submitting such a report you grant us permission to contribute the place information in it, including any description of the place you write, to OpenStreetMap, where it is published under the Open Database License (ODbL) in accordance with the OpenStreetMap Foundation’s Contributor Terms. To the extent your description is protected by copyright, you license it to us for this purpose, and you agree that it may be published under those terms.

When you submit a report, you confirm that:

  • it is true to the best of your knowledge, and based on your own observation or knowledge of the place;
  • you have not copied it from another map, website, guide or database (for example, Google Maps), whose terms usually forbid this and whose data OpenStreetMap cannot accept;
  • it contains no personal data — neither yours nor anyone else’s — in the name or description of the place.

Things to know before you submit:

  • Every report is reviewed. Reports are checked automatically and then by a person, who may correct, shorten or reject them, and who decides whether the change is written to OpenStreetMap or left for local mappers as a public note. We are under no obligation to accept or publish a report.
  • Only place data is contributed. Names, categories, opening hours, fees, accessibility, descriptions and similar attributes of a place. We never contribute your photos, Wi-Fi credentials, your name, your account, your note to us, or any other personal data.
  • Contributions are made under our own OpenStreetMap account, not in your name. Nothing identifies you in the public map.
  • You can withdraw a report until it is reviewed. Once it has been accepted and published on OpenStreetMap, it cannot be withdrawn: the data is copied and redistributed worldwide by others. Deleting your account does not remove it from OpenStreetMap.

If you would rather not have a particular observation contributed, do not submit it as a report. This section applies only to reports and proposed places; it does not apply to the private POIs you keep for yourself.
