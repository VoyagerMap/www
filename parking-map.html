Why parking is still a problem in an unfamiliar town

  • Arriving somewhere new, the first question is rarely the destination itself — it is where to leave the car.
  • Whether a car park is free, paid, covered, or open at all is often missing from ordinary map results.
  • Circling a full city centre wastes fuel and time that a nearby alternative would have saved.

Built for drivers who need a spot, not a detour

Parking map for road trips, city breaks and daily driving

A parking map is one of those tools you only think about at the moment you need it, usually while circling an unfamiliar town centre. Voyager Maps carries 1,174,855 parking locations worldwide — car parks, multi-storey and underground garages, and the roadside stops that ordinary maps tend to skip — so the question of where to leave the car has an answer before you arrive.

Cost is usually the deciding factor, and 810,544 of those locations are recorded as free of charge. For a road trip, a van life route or a weekend in a city you do not know, being able to filter the map down to parking alone, and to see what is free nearby, turns a stressful few minutes into a decision you make in advance.
