Campervan and motorhome service points for van life, road trips and long tours

Campervan travel runs on a short list of essentials: fresh water, a working shower, somewhere to wash clothes, and a pitch for the night. Finding them is rarely the relaxed part of the trip. Service points for motorhomes and campers are documented unevenly — some on club sites behind a membership, some in forum threads years out of date, some only in a language you do not read. The result is that experienced travelers often plan their route around the few places they already trust, rather than the many that exist nearby.

A campervan service map is more useful when it starts from open map data instead of member submissions, because coverage then reflects what is actually on the ground rather than who happened to post about it. Voyager Maps brings campsites, caravan sites, drinking water points, showers, self-service laundries, toilets, fuel and parking into one searchable map, so you can check what is near tonight's stop before you drive there. Whether you are touring full time, taking a long summer trip, or spending a weekend away in a converted van, the practical stops are the ones worth having on hand.
