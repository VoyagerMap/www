Finding a toilet, a tap or a shower in Warsaw

General map apps are built around businesses and directions, so the free and public things — a toilet in a park, a drinking fountain on a square, a shower at a beach — are the ones they cover least well. That is the gap this map fills. In Warsaw it currently holds 407 public toilets and 184 places to refill a bottle, each with whatever the source records about cost, opening hours and access.
